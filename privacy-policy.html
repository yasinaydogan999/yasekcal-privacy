YASEKCAL Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how YASEKCAL ("YASEKCAL", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the YASEKCAL mobile application.

Important: In the current version of YASEKCAL, food photos used for AI food analysis are processed locally on your device. YASEKCAL does not upload or collect those photos on our servers for AI model training.

1. Information and permissions

YASEKCAL may request access to device features that are necessary to provide app functionality, including the camera for capturing food images and notification permission for reminders or other notifications you choose to enable.

2. Food photos and AI analysis

Food images captured or selected for YASEKCAL's food-recognition functionality are currently processed locally on the user's device. In the current version, these food images are not uploaded to YASEKCAL servers and are not collected by YASEKCAL for training AI models.

If a future version introduces an optional feature that allows users to contribute food images to improve YASEKCAL's AI models, this Privacy Policy and the relevant in-app disclosures and consent controls will be updated before that collection is introduced.

3. Advertising

YASEKCAL may display advertising to non-Premium users using Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, interactions with advertisements, and other device or usage information in accordance with their own policies and applicable law.

Where required, YASEKCAL uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising-related privacy choices and consent. The availability and type of advertising may depend on your region and privacy choices.

4. Premium subscriptions and purchases

YASEKCAL may offer Premium subscriptions through Google Play. Purchases and payments are processed by Google Play. YASEKCAL does not receive your full payment-card details. Information necessary to determine or verify subscription entitlement may be processed in connection with providing Premium access.

5. Notifications

If you enable notifications, YASEKCAL may use the notification permission to provide features such as meal or water reminders. You can change notification permissions through your device settings.

6. Third-party services

YASEKCAL uses or may use third-party services required to operate specific features, including Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), and Google Play Billing. These providers may process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Data retention and security

Because food-photo AI analysis in the current version occurs locally, YASEKCAL does not retain those food photos on its servers for AI analysis or model training. Information processed by third-party providers is subject to those providers' retention and security practices. We take reasonable measures appropriate to the information and functionality under our control.

8. Your privacy choices

Where applicable, users can make advertising privacy choices through the consent interface provided in YASEKCAL. Device permissions such as camera and notifications can also be managed through the operating system settings. Where required by applicable law, additional rights may be available regarding personal data processed about you.

9. Children's privacy

YASEKCAL is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If we learn that personal information has been collected from a child in circumstances requiring parental consent without the required authorization, we will take appropriate steps to address it.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when YASEKCAL's features, data practices, legal requirements, or third-party services change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when the policy is revised. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law.
